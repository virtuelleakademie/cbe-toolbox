## Challenge & Stakeholders

### Challenge Topic

Smart Road Management

### Challenge Formulation

The aim of the project is to create a technological basis for an efficient system for the maintenance and upkeep of the streets of the city of Biel. For this purpose, basic data on road damage is collected and processed. The intelligent and application-oriented system is intended to connect infrastructure data and condition analyses and, in a final step, make the results available via an app.

### Challenge Authenticity / Origin

Real partner brief combined with co-design involving instructors (participatory design research).

### Challenge Owner / Stakeholders

BFH TI / BFH TI (Data Engineering), City of Biel (Road Inspectorate)

### Stakeholder Involvement Moments

Kick-off briefing, data provision, final presentation, implementation (if applicable)

## Constructive Alignment

### Intended Learning Outcomes

1. Knowledge and Understanding: They are familiar with the principles of systemic thinking and approaches.

2. Application and Analysis: They are able to work in a team to overcome challenges and find solutions together.

3. Judgement and Problem-Solving: They have learned to independently design, implement, and present a solution to a problem as a team.

### Key Learning Activities

Problem-based learning; cooperative learning; group work; self-study; presentation (at the end of the week)

Specifically:

Train a CNN for object detection to identify road damage on the streets of Biel.

Dealing with a large training dataset (Road Damage Dataset (RDD)).

Training on the Machine Learning Management Platform (MLMP).

Creating a test dataset on the streets of Biel for the final evaluation.

### Assessment Criteria / Rubrics

The winning team is the team with the highest mAP@0.5 (mean Average Precision @ 0.5 or higher Intersection over Union).

### Assessment Evidence & Feedback

At the end of the week, the project demonstration takes place (proof of competence) & stakeholder feedback (Road Inspectorate of the City of Biel)

### Reflection Design

Daily meeting with the coaches about work in progress (online and in-presence), presentation

## Phase-by-Phase Walkthrough

### Phase 1 – Engage

Plenary session (introduction), followed by team building

Each team: Reading, RDD Analysis, who is doing what, Planning

RDD Harmonization: Analysis, Resizing, Annotation, Synchronisation

### Phase 2 – Investigate

Model Training: Model Analysis, Scripting, MLMP, Training, Evaluation, Tuning

### Phase 3 – Act / Prototype

Test Dataset Creation: Collecting Damage Photos, Annotation, Synchronisation

### Phase 4 – Reflect & Present

Competition: Test Set Adaptation, Presentation, Final Test Set Evaluation
