# Glossary and FAQ

Product terms and short answers.

<a id="glossary"></a>

<!-- topic:glossary -->

## Glossary

- **Course:** the local workspace for one module, including Chats, design state and course Wiki.
- **conversation / Chat:** the sequence of your requests and assistant responses in a Course.
- **New Session:** a fresh conversational context that keeps the Course's recorded design and older stored Chat.
- **CBE Process Canvas:** the ten-step record of settled design components.
- **card:** one recorded component on the Canvas.
- **focus:** the design step currently being worked on.
- **depth:** how far a step has been worked: open, rough, refined or detailed.
- **CBE Maturity:** a working assessment of the recorded design against the seven PIONEER maturity criteria.
- **target:** the intended overall ambition or the desired level for a criterion.
- **overall achieved:** the overall level the app derives from the current levels recorded for the criteria.
- **criterion:** one of the seven PIONEER dimensions used by the Maturity assessment.
- **corpus:** curated CBE methodology delivered with the Toolbox.
- **course Wiki:** reusable material imported for one Course.
- **atom:** one short, searchable note the Toolbox creates from material imported into the Wiki, with its source and related notes.
- **attachment:** a local file supplied to the next conversation turn, not automatically added to the Wiki.
- **citation:** a clickable reference to a corpus document or Course-Wiki atom.
- **support preference:** your explicit lasting choice for guided, collaborative or concise assistance; not an estimate of competence and not consent to formulate content.
- **reasoning:** a provider/model setting controlling supported reasoning effort; it is not the visible explanation in the answer and not a CBE work mode.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to complete the ten steps in order?

No. Start at your relevant decision; open fields remain visible. The coherence check once steps 1–3 are completed still matters. See [CBE Process Canvas](user-guide.md#canvas).

### Should I attach a file or import it into the Wiki?

Attach for the next turn; import for reusable Course material. See [Attachment or course Wiki?](user-guide.md#attachment-versus-wiki).

### Does choosing guided support let the assistant write my module?

No. Preference controls assistance style, not authorship consent. See [Choose the support you want](user-guide.md#support-and-consent) and [Formulation help remains your decision](user-guide.md#iterative-formulation).

### Where are my files and what should I back up?

See [What is stored locally](privacy-data-storage.md#local-storage) and the [Back up one Course or the whole installation](privacy-data-storage.md#backup-boundary).

### Is an uncited suggestion verified CBE methodology?

No. It can be useful working material but must remain non-authoritative. See [sources and citations](user-guide.md#sources-and-citations).

### Can I use personal student data?

No. Remove or anonymise it before chat, attachment or Wiki ingestion. See [Do not submit personal student data](privacy-data-storage.md#student-data).

### Where do I get the current installer?

Download it from [Installation for end users](installation-and-local-it.md#install-current-build).

### How do I recover from a visible error?

Keep the original work and use the symptom-first [Common symptoms and safe recovery](troubleshooting-and-reference.md#troubleshooting).
